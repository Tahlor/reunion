Self-guided Provo River float
Put-in mapTake-out map
Safety and logistics
Bring your own kayaks, tubes or raft, paddles and properly fitted life jackets. This is not an outfitter trip, so there is no equipment rental or commercial shuttle included.
Put-in: Lower Provo River Park, immediately below Deer Creek Dam.
Take-out: Vivian Park,
6828 South Fork Road, Provo, UT 84606
.
The standard lower-river float is roughly 5–6 miles and commonly takes about 1–1½ hours depending on flow. Stage enough vehicles at Vivian Park before launching, shuttle drivers back to the put-in, and retrieve the put-in vehicles afterward. Lower Provo River Park has a Deer Creek State Park day-use fee.
Safety: Utah requires every person on a river vessel—including an inner tube—to wear a properly sized U.S. Coast Guard-approved life jacket. The water is about 45°F, secure river shoes are strongly recommended, and the angled supports at the Wildwood railroad trestle are the principal obstacle on this stretch. Check the day’s river flow and weather before launching.
